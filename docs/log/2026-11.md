### Stage 1: the first `[ew]` reader on this fleet

*2026-11-22*

Until now `[ew]` was written (PerceptLearn, `@LAT91`) and read by nothing. Since ACT-III C3
the Cardputer no longer writes `@LAT91` at all, so the writer is dormant on current
firmware. `ttdbParseEw()` (TtdbParse) and `fleet.parse_ew()` are a pair driven by the same
28 vectors (`tests/vectors/ew_vectors.txt`). They add `prec` (default 128; `prec:0` means
*ignore*, and `has_prec` tells it from absent). RFC-0005 leaves two rules open, decided
here: a repeated key's last occurrence wins, and an unterminated block is no block. No
behaviour changed and nothing was flashed.

### PW-1b: re-deriving `pe_span` — INCONCLUSIVE; acoustic was span-limited, motion and link are quiet

*2026-11-22*

PW-1 left acoustic, motion and link at `π` ≈ 250–255. PW-1b re-ran it with only `pe_span`
changed, set to the firmware's own state-change constants: acoustic 900, motion 60 mg.
Link fell back to its clamp domain (90 dB), because its best constant
(`PERCEPTLEARN_RSSI_BAND 6`) was itself measured on this archive. Result: acoustic 241 (not
saturated), motion 251 and link 249 (saturated). The verdict is INCONCLUSIVE on one series:
`v4a/link` crossed the split-half tolerance, 23 → 28.

⚠ **The acoustic result is the Cardputer's alone** (227, drifting). The K10's ear folds to
255 at every span tried. Stage 3 uses PW-1b's spans, as registered.

⚠ **PW-1 and PW-1b read lanes 94–97 only, so neither contains Cardputer data after
2026-10-02**, when ACT-III C0 moved the percept tiers into `@LAT103` episodes. Stage 3 folds
episodes, so its gate needs an episode reader of its own (not a change to `tools/pw1`).
