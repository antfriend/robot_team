### Stage 1: the first `[ew]` reader on this fleet

*2026-11-22*

Until now `[ew]` was written (PerceptLearn, `@LAT91`) and read by nothing. Since ACT-III C3
the Cardputer no longer writes `@LAT91` at all, so the writer is dormant on current
firmware. `ttdbParseEw()` (TtdbParse) and `fleet.parse_ew()` are a pair driven by the same
28 vectors (`tests/vectors/ew_vectors.txt`). They add `prec` (default 128; `prec:0` means
*ignore*, and `has_prec` tells it from absent). RFC-0005 leaves two rules open, decided
here: a repeated key's last occurrence wins, and an unterminated block is no block. No
behaviour changed and nothing was flashed.

### RFC-0011 stage 3: `@LAT108` on the Cardputer, PASSED on run 3 (two runs failed first)

*2026-11-22*

Stage 3 writes one PRECISION record per channel (link, entity, motion, acoustic), folded
from flash in Stage D. It is observed only; nothing reads `π`. Spec and every change to it:
`docs/design/rfc0011-stage3.md` §1–§12. Gate: `tools/stage3/stage3_gate.py`, which reuses

| run | build | what failed | what it forced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `424873b` synchronous | fold took 141–288 ms (budget 100); acoustic UNVERIFIABLE in pull 2 | D3b (a job, 2 episodes a pass), D7 (rewrite when `from` moves) |
| 2 | `3b86d6f` | each rewrite took **~3 s** (`removeCuts` = whole-file rewrite of ~185 KB); acoustic UNVERIFIABLE again | D8 (a cut rider: the record leaves with its episodes), D9, D10 |
| 3 | `7059962` | as run: acoustic FAIL, a **gate-tool defect** (identical episode, trailing `---`) | accepted by Dan under the corrected tool |

The rules these runs paid for:
- ⚠ **"First write" timings say nothing about rewrites.** Run 1's 58–122 ms were appends. A
  rewrite of this ~185 KB TTDB costs ~3 s and shows as a `linkperc` worst pass of the same
  size. Never budget a "write" without saying which kind.
- ⚠ **A record derived from episodes must leave flash with them.** Acoustic writes ~1 episode
  a minute against a quota of 24, so any refold cadence slower than the cut loses the range.
  `EpisodeNode::attachCutRider` lets a derived lane add its deletions to the tier's own
  `removeCuts`. That is the general fix for any future lane that cites episodes.
- ⚠ **`catchboot.py` buffers to exit.** A live watch on it sees nothing, and a crash loses the
  log. Use `tools/stage3/holdlog.py` (line-flushed, optional reset) for any held window.
- ⚠ **`stage3_gate.records()` runs a record to the next header**, so text comparisons between
  pulls must strip the trailing `---`. That is what produced run 3's false FAIL.
- Cost against pre-stage-3: flash +9.0 KB, static RAM +1.4 KB (the fold state, static since
  D3b). The worst append in run 3 was 186 ms at 15.6 KB free heap, 14 ms under its limit.
