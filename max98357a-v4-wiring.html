MAX98357A → Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4

An I²S 3 W Class-D amp gives the LoRa spine a voice — so the V4-A bridge, the band's timekeeper, can play its downbeats instead of flashing a silent LED. Five signal wires plus a speaker.

Before you solder

  • ✦GAIN & SD have no wire. Leave both floating: GAIN floating sets 9 dB, SD floating keeps the amp on and sums to mono. Tie SD to 3V3 if you want a guaranteed-on rail; pull it low to mute.
  • ①Verify GPIO 5/6/7 on your V4 header against the official pinmap before soldering — they're free (LoRa SPI is 8–14, OLED is 17/18/21) and match the T-Deck's I²S pins, but confirm they're broken out and avoid the strapping pins 0/3/45/46.
  • ②3V3 works everywhere. The amp runs 2.7–5.5 V, and the V4's 3V3 rail is live on both USB and battery — so every V4 wires identically and a field node still has a voice off-grid. It gives ~1.3 W into 4 Ω, plenty for a toot. If a tone ever "sags" on loud hits, add a 100–470 µF cap across the amp's VIN/GND.
  • ③Firmware is nearly free. Lift toneI2S() from the T-Deck console, gate it behind USE_SPEAKER, set the pins with gI2S.setPins(7, 5, 6) (BCLK, WS, DIN), and swap the bridge's REST part for real downbeat notes.
